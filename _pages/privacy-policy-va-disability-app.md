## Privacy Policy

### **1\. Introduction**

VA Disability Calculator (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

### **2\. Information We Collect**

*   **User-Provided Data:** All disability and dependent entries are stored locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit any PII or PHI off your device.
    
*   **Analytics:** We do not integrate any third-party analytics or crash reporters.
    

### **3\. How We Use Your Data**

Your data stays on your device:

*   To calculate your combined VA rating and estimated compensation.
    
*   To persist your settings between launches.
    

### **4\. Data Sharing**

We do **not** share, sell, or transmit any of your personal data to third parties.

### **5\. Security**

Data is stored unencrypted in a local JSON file. You are solely responsible for device-level security (PIN, Touch/Face ID).

### **6\. Children’s Privacy**

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

### **7\. Changes to This Policy**

We may update this policy when VA rules or app features change. Changes will be posted here with a revised “Last updated” date.

### **8\. Contact Us**

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the [contact page](/contact/).
