Version 1.26.8 of the [VA Compensation Calculator](/va-compensation-calculator/) is rolling out on the App Store. This one is a focused bug-fix and accuracy update — no new screens to learn.

What changed:

  - **Improved accuracy of combined ratings and monthly compensation.** Several calculation edge cases now line up exactly with 38 CFR — including the bilateral factor and dependents who are children 18–23 in school. A small number of estimates will shift slightly as a result.
  - **Claim Tracker events are now saved reliably.** Events you added stay put across app restarts.
  - **Updated 38 CFR Diagnostic Codes to the latest VA schedule.** Digestive-system criteria, cranial nerve codes, and the rating bands now match the current regulation.
  - **Various bug fixes and improvements.**

## Free, ad-free, private. Same as it's always been.

Nothing in this release changes how your data is handled. Everything is still stored locally on your device with complete file protection — no analytics, no accounts. The Tip Jar is optional and unlocks nothing.
