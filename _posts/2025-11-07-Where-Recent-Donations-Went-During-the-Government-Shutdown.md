I’d like to thank everyone who chose to donate through my VA Disability Calculator app. During the recent government shutdown, those funds were used to purchase food items for a local shelter. All donations were matched dollar-for-dollar, converted directly into supplies, and delivered in person.

My kids assisted with the drop-off as a way to learn about community support and service. I’m sharing this to show where the support went and to highlight why local, direct aid matters during disruptions like this.
