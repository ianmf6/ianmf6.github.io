Version 1.26.3 of the [VA Compensation Calculator](/va-disability-calculator-ios-app/) is now available on the App Store.

## What's New

### Track Pending and Denied Claims

You can now track pending and denied claims right alongside your rated disabilities. The disabilities list adds a status filter so you can switch between **Rated**, **Pending**, and **Denied** views — useful when you want to focus on what's currently counted toward your combined rating versus what's still in motion.

### Projected Rating

A new **Projected Rating** view shows what your combined rating could become if your pending claims are approved, giving you a forward-looking number to plan around. When sharing your breakdown, you can also choose to include pending claims so the projection travels with the share.

### Year Picker in Settings

The compensation year picker is now also available in **Settings**, so you can change rate years from either the main flow or the settings screen — whichever is closer to where you are.

### Refinements

A handful of smaller improvements round out the release:

  - The Spouse Aid & Attendance section is hidden when you're not married, removing irrelevant fields from the dependents flow.
  - Filtering with no matching results now shows a clear empty-state message instead of a blank list.
  - The Help section has been reorganized into clearer sub-sections so answers are easier to find.
  - "What's New" is shown automatically after each update so you always know what changed.

---

As always, all data remains stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to external servers. Claim status, projected ratings, and share contents stay under your control — nothing leaves the app without your action.
