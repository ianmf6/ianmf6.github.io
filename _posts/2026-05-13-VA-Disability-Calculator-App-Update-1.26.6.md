Version 1.26.6 of the [VA Compensation Calculator](/va-disability-calculator-ios-app/) is now available on the App Store. This is a big update — new tools, new tracking, new privacy features, and a refreshed layout.

## What's New

### Claim Tracker

Track upcoming **C&P exams, hearings, deadlines, and decision dates** in one place, with optional reminders so you don't miss a date. Everything stays on your device — no calendar sync or external sharing.

### What If? Scenario Builder

Interactively explore how rating changes affect your combined rating and monthly compensation **without touching your saved data**. Bump a percentage up or down, add a hypothetical condition, and see the impact in real time.

### 38 CFR Diagnostic Codes

A searchable in-app reference covering **~300 diagnostic codes across 15 body systems**. When adding a disability, the diagnostic-code search fills the name and suggests common ratings (tinnitus, for example, auto-fills at 10%).

### TDIU Support

New schedular **Eligibility Check** for Total Disability based on Individual Unemployability. A Settings toggle pays compensation at the **100% rate** when TDIU is granted, so your monthly estimate reflects what you'll actually receive.

### Save as PDF

Export your **Compensation Breakdown** or **Backpay** results as a styled PDF — handy for sharing with a VSO, an attorney, or for your own records.

### Notes per Disability

Capture **effective dates, decision letter references, or symptom history** alongside each disability entry. Useful for keeping context in one place when you have a complex history.

### Privacy & Security

  - **Biometric lock** — optionally require Face ID or Touch ID to open the app.
  - Saved data is now **encrypted while your device is locked** (iOS Complete File Protection).

### Dark Mode

System / Light / Dark, selectable in **Settings → Appearance**.

### Refreshed Layout

  - **Help (?)** and **Settings (gear)** icons now live in the top-right of every screen for one-tap access.
  - A dedicated **Help** screen consolidates How to Use, What's New, Support & Community, Rate & Review, and Tip Jar.
  - A new **Calculators & References** section on the main screen gathers the Backpay Calculator, Pay Date Predictor, Combined Ratings Table, Healthcare Priority Group, Future COLA Estimator, and CRDP / CRSC alongside the new tools.
  - The **Disabilities** screen now has a search bar and new sort options.
  - A **quick orientation sheet** appears on first launch and after this update so the new layout is easy to find your way around.

### Tip Jar

Four optional tip tiers — **$2 / $5 / $10 / $20** — if you'd like to support development. The app stays free and ad-free forever, and **tipping unlocks no features**.

### Refinements

  - Backpay now accepts future dates for projecting upcoming retroactive pay.
  - Sorting the Disabilities list no longer reorders your saved data.
  - Backpay screen reworded for clarity.
  - Various small UI improvements and bug fixes.

---

As always, all data remains stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to external servers. The new biometric lock and on-device encryption are entirely user-controlled — nothing leaves the app without your action.
