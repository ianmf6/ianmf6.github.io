Version 1.26.10 of the [VA Compensation Calculator](/va-compensation-calculator/) is rolling out on the App Store. This one adds a **Symptom Log** for building claim evidence over time, fills out the **Special Monthly Compensation** ladder, makes **Backpay** handle life changes, and moves feedback out of a Google form and into the app.

## Symptom Log — evidence, not a verdict

Many conditions are rated on how *often* something happens and how *long* it lasts — migraines on the frequency of prostrating attacks (DC 8100), epilepsy on seizure frequency, asthma on attack frequency. If it only flares once a month, it's hard to prove months later at a C&P exam.

The new **Symptom Log** lets you record each episode as it happens — start time, duration, severity, symptoms, whether you sought care, and whether it was prostrating. The app then summarizes it in the same language the rating criteria use ("6 episodes · 4 prostrating · ~2 / month · last 90 days") and lets you share a clean report as text or PDF with your VSO or provider.

To be clear about what this is: the app **logs and summarizes — it never rates or diagnoses.** Summaries may reference rating-criteria language for context, but they always present your own data, not a determination.

- Log episodes for migraines, seizures, breathing or GI flare-ups, mental-health episodes, chronic-pain flares, and more.
- Open a condition's log straight from its row in My Disabilities, or from the main **Symptom Log** screen.
- Logging a brand-new condition? You can optionally add it to My Disabilities (Pending, 0%) so it's ready to track — and search the 38 CFR diagnostic codes right there to tag it correctly.
- Your episodes and their summaries stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

## The full Special Monthly Compensation ladder

SMC now covers the complete awardable ladder — **K, S, L, L½, M, M½, N, N½, O/P, R.1, R.2, and T** — with the intermediate levels added this release. Rates were sourced from VA.gov for both 2025 and 2026 and cross-checked against the levels the app already supported, so estimates at the higher and in-between levels are more accurate.

## Backpay that follows your life

Retroactive awards often span a stretch where your household changed — a marriage, a new child. Backpay now lets you add **dated life events** so the estimate uses the right dependent rate for each part of the period, instead of assuming today's dependents applied the whole time.

## Contact Me — off the Google form

The old "Send Feedback" link bounced you to a Google form in a browser. It's now a native **Contact Me** screen that sends straight to my own server — no Google, no third-party data mining. Report a bug, ask a question, or suggest a feature without leaving the app, and add an email only if you'd like a reply.

## Also

Various bug fixes and improvements throughout.

As always: your VA information stays on your device and under your control. If something's off or you have an idea, tap **?** → **Contact Me** and tell me.
