Version 1.26.2 of the [VA Compensation Calculator](/va-compensation-calculator/) is now available on the App Store.

## What's New

### Share Your Rating Breakdown

You can now share your compensation breakdown directly from the app. Before sharing, a **Share Options screen** gives you control over what's included — toggle off disability names to redact them from the output, or exclude dependent information entirely. The share uses the native iOS share sheet, so your data goes only where you send it.

### Cleaner Compensation Breakdown

The breakdown view has been reorganized for clarity — less visual noise, tighter layout, and clearer wording throughout so the numbers are easier to follow at a glance.

### Smarter Input Validation

Adding disabilities now catches invalid entries earlier with more helpful feedback, reducing the friction of correcting mistakes mid-entry.

### Bug Fix: Combined Percentage Before Rounding

Fixed an issue where the breakdown displayed an incorrect combined percentage before rounding was applied. The value now accurately reflects the unrounded figure used in the VA's incremental calculation.

---

As always, all data remains stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to external servers. The share feature is entirely user-initiated — nothing leaves the app without your action.
